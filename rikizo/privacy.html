Privacy Policy

The Adventures of Rikizo, by Edison Studios LLC
Effective date: October 27, 2026

The short version: The Adventures of Rikizo runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no third-party services.

Who we are

The Adventures of Rikizo ("the game") is developed and published by Edison Studios LLC ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the game on every platform where it is offered, including Google Play (Android) and the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad).

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect any personal information or usage data, from anyone. Specifically, the game:

  • does not require or offer an account or sign-in;
  • does not show advertising and contains no advertising software;
  • does not include analytics, crash reporting, tracking, or any other third-party software development kits (SDKs);
  • does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos;
  • does not connect to the internet or send any information off your device;
  • does not offer in-app purchases.

Saved games

Your progress and settings are saved in local storage on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This save data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it. Depending on your device settings, your operating system's own device backup features (for example, Android or iCloud device backups) may include app data; those backups are handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies, not by us.

Sharing of information

Because we do not collect any information, we have nothing to sell, share, or disclose to third parties.

App stores

When you download the game, the store you use (Google Play or the Apple App Store) may process information about you under its own privacy policy. We do not receive personal information about individual players from those stores beyond standard, aggregated sales and download reports.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The game collects no data from any player of any age.

If you contact us

If you choose to email us, we will receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you, and we do not share it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
