Reporting a bug

To help us track a problem down quickly, please include:

  • your device model (for example, "Pixel 7 Pro" or "iPhone 15");
  • your operating system version (Android or iOS/iPadOS version);
  • the game version (shown in the store listing);
  • roughly where you were in the game (which in-game day, and what you were doing);
  • a screenshot, if you can take one.

Saves and progress

The game runs fully offline and saves your progress only on your device. We have no copy of your save, so uninstalling the game or clearing its data will erase your progress.
